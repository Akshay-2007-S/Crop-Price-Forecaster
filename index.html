BENCHMARK EVALUATION

Forecast Model Comparison

Prototype evaluation comparing baseline heuristics against deep time-series and gradient-boosted ensembles

BASELINE

7-Day Moving Average

Simple / Exponential Moving Average (SMA/EMA) smoothing historical window.

MAE (Error) ₹3.82 / kg
RMSE ₹4.91 / kg
Verdict: Lags behind rapid weather shocks and festival demand surges by 4–6 days.
TIME-SERIES MODEL

ARIMA / Prophet

Auto-Regressive Integrated Moving Average with additive holiday seasonality components.

MAE (Error) ₹2.14 / kg
RMSE ₹2.85 / kg
Verdict: Robust for predictable seasonal waves; fails to model sudden district transport strikes or floods.
RECOMMENDED AI ENGINE ML ENSEMBLE MODEL

Random Forest + XGBoost

Multi-modal gradient boosted trees integrating spatial mandi lag, IMD rain, and crowdsourced sentiment.

MAE (Error) ₹1.48 / kg
RMSE ₹1.95 / kg
Verdict: Lowest error spread across volatile commodities (Tomato, Chilli). Evaluated on 2024-25 APMC dataset.
